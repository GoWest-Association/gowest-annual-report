Federal Advocacy

Proactive Policy Impact

Successfully influenced critical legislation through behind-the-scenes engagement with key congressional leaders and staff on issues including fraud and scams, mortgage lending flexibilities, stablecoin and digital assets parity, operating environment modernization, and the repeal of harmful rules.

Protecting Against Policy Threats

Defended against harmful legislative proposals, including attempts to tax credit unions through the budget reconciliation and tax packages, reduce interchange revenue, and undermine the CDFI program.

Strengthening Relationships with Policymakers

Met with all 48 members of our Congressional delegation and key committee staff to advocate on crucial public policy issues impacting credit unions and nurture relationships and support.

Advancing CU Act & Financial Services Modernization

Secured passage of the Credit Union Board Modernization Act, co-sponsored by all 12 GoWest Senators. Introduced or advanced legislation to strengthen fraud protections, modernize reporting thresholds, expand mortgage and business lending flexibility, improve deposit insurance and data security parity, increase loan maturity limits, support credit unions’ authorities around digital assets, and strengthen CDFI funding.

Election Engagement & Funding Support

Mobilized grassroots engagement and financial support for credit union champions, including executing a member communications campaign and piloting grassroots canvassing in competitive districts, 30+ credit union fundraisers, support for 38 delegation members, and extensive candidate interviews of future congressional leaders.

State Advocacy

Arizona

  • Passed significant modernization updates to Arizona State Credit Union Charter.
  • Defeated interchange legislation.
  • Defeated credit card interest rate cap bill.
  • Secured veto of bill that'd create legal, operational, and service issues for credit unions.

Colorado

  • Defeated harmful interchange legislation.
  • Protected credit union interests in motor vehicle lending, consumer protection, and business practices proposed policies.
  • Passed ASSET Act legislation to protect seniors from fraud.
  • Advocated for credit union interests in artificial intelligence and surveillance pricing legislation.

Idaho

  • Defeated the Idaho Department of Finance and Department of Insurance merger.
  • Secured additional leniency when it comes to turning titles over to the Idaho Department of Transportation.
  • Continued to work toward policies to provide additional resources on fraud prevention measures.

Oregon

  • Protected Oregon credit unions’ tax status in difficult budget discussions during short session.
  • Advocated for credit unions in legislative hearings on housing, lending, and consumer protection.
  • Led efforts to educate elected officials on credit union efforts to prevent financial fraud and protect members.
  • Educated candidates and legislators throughout the state on the structure and unique work of Oregon credit unions ahead of the upcoming long session.

Washington

  • Protected state credit union tax exemption amidst a historic budget deficit and elimination of other similar tax treatment.
  • Secured broad exemptions for financial institutions from state AI regulation proposals.
  • Protected consumers and lenders through legal advocacy challenging a problematic state Supreme Court ruling, which was ultimately withdrawn.

Wyoming

  • Passed virtual currency kiosk legislation to address fraud.
  • Supported Uniform Mortgage Modification Act to streamline mortgage modifications while protecting lien priority.
  • Amended anti-money laundering legislation to exempt credit unions from additional reporting requirements.
  • Amended and supported portable benefit account legislation.

Credit Union Education & Networking

  • Connecting Credit Union Leaders
    In 2026, 1,430 credit union leaders participated in GoWest’s five signature events. Attendees come from across organizations, from CEOs and executives to board members, young professionals, and industry partners. Through strategic dialogue, leadership development, peer networking, and exposure to new solutions, attendees found a place to learn, connect, and shape the future of the Movement.

  • Talent Development
    From specialized training and regulatory education to leadership development, GoWest equips professionals at every level with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to strengthen their credit unions.

  • Committees & Advisory Group
    Through GoWest Committees and Advisory Groups, hundreds of credit union professionals share their perspectives, experience, and insights to help shape the Association’s initiatives, advocacy, communications, and community impact. New for 2027, a Fraud Prevention & Cyber Resilience Task Force will help guide collaboration and peer support to protect the credit union system.

  • CU Communities
    Across the GoWest region, 20 Chapters, six Young Professional Groups, and 10 Global Women’s Leadership Network Sister Societies bring together hundreds of credit union professionals to grow as leaders, turn shared purpose into local impact and strengthen the Movement.

  • Councils
    Build a network of support across our six-state region through specialized Councils, connecting with peers and industry experts to exchange ideas, tackle challenges, and bring actionable takeaways back to your credit union. Engagement included more than 2,200 registrations by 924 individuals representing 119 credit unions.

  • Small Credit Union Accelerator
    Guided by an Advisory Council of small credit union CEOs, small credit unions can access free and discounted programming, monthly virtual roundtables, in-person summits, and tailored expert support designed to help credit unions of all sizes thrive.

  • CU Learning Partners
    Credit Union Learning Partners is a new GoWest program that brings together small groups of credit union professionals for focused, peer-based learning around shared challenges and areas of focus. Participants gain a curated network of relevant peers and a platform to solve specific challenges at their credit union.
